# Lab journal

Newest entries at the top. Screenshots go in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/).

Remove tenant IDs, real email addresses and passwords from screenshots before committing.

## Template (copy this block for each entry)

### YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>

**Goal:** what I wanted to do.
**What I ran:** script name and parameters, or portal steps.
**Result:** what happened (counts, objects created, report produced).
**Problem met and fix:** error message, cause, how I solved it.
**What I learned:** one or two lines.
**Evidence:** `screenshots/YYYY-MM-DD-title.png`
